## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
Detailed Explanation of the Proposed Solution
AgroConnect is an AI-powered digital marketplace that directly connects farmers and FPOs with consumers and bulk buyers, reducing dependency on multiple intermediaries.
Farmers can list their agricultural products, quantities and prices, while buyers can directly browse and place orders through the platform.
The system also provides logistics support for delivery and uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization, helping reduce transportation costs, food wastage and supply chain inefficiencies.
The platform aims to provide better prices for farmers and affordable agricultural products for consumers.

AgroConnect reduces the involvement of multiple intermediaries by directly connecting farmers with consumers and bulk buyers. It ensures better price transparency, provides logistics support for efficient delivery and uses AI to predict demand and optimize routes. This helps increase farmers' earnings, reduce consumer prices and minimize food wastage.

AgroConnect introduces an innovative approach by combining a direct farmer-to-consumer marketplace with AI-powered demand forecasting and smart logistics management in a single platform. Unlike traditional agricultural supply chains, it reduces dependency on multiple intermediaries and promotes transparent pricing.

The platform uses AI to predict crop demand based on previous sales and seasonal trends, helping farmers make better decisions about production and sales. It also provides route optimization to reduce transportation costs and delivery delays.

Its uniqueness lies in integrating direct trading, intelligent demand prediction and efficient transportation into one digital platform, creating a smarter and more transparent agricultural supply chain.

## Technical Approach

1. Technologies to Be Used
- Frontend: HTML, CSS and JavaScript for designing an interactive and user-friendly website.
- Backend: Python and Django for handling user registration, product listings and orders.
- Database: SQLite or MySQL for storing farmer, buyer, product and order details.
- Artificial Intelligence: Python libraries such as Pandas and Scikit-learn for demand forecasting.
- Route Optimization: Google Maps API for location tracking and finding efficient delivery routes.
- Development Tools: Visual Studio Code and GitHub for coding and project management.

2. Methodology and Process for Implementation
The AgroConnect platform will be developed through the following stages:
1. User Registration: Farmers, FPOs and buyers register and create their profiles.
2. Product Listing: Farmers upload crop details, available quantities and expected prices.
3. Order Management: Buyers browse products, compare prices and place orders directly.
4. Logistics Management: The system coordinates transportation, delivery scheduling and route optimization.
5. AI Demand Forecasting: AI analyses historical sales and seasonal trends to predict future crop demand.
6. Delivery and Tracking: Buyers can track their orders until deliver

## Feasibility and Viability
1. Analysis of the Feasibility of the Idea
AgroConnect is technically and economically feasible as it can be developed using existing technologies such as Python, Django, HTML, CSS and JavaScript. AI libraries can be used for demand forecasting, while mapping services can support route optimization.

The platform can initially be developed as a web application with minimal infrastructure costs and later expanded to support more farmers, buyers and transportation providers. Its implementation is practical, scalable and suitable for improving the agricultural supply chain.

Potential Challenges and Risks
- Digital Literacy: Some farmers may have limited knowledge of using digital platforms.
- Internet Connectivity: Poor internet access in rural areas may affect platform usage.
- Data Accuracy: Incorrect sales and demand data may affect AI predictions.
- Logistics Issues: Transportation delays, vehicle availability and delivery costs may create difficulties.
- Product Quality: Maintaining freshness and quality during transportation can be challenging

Strategies for Overcoming These Challenges
- Simple User Interface: Provide an easy-to-use platform with regional language support to help farmers access services.
- Offline Support: Allow basic product details to be saved and updated when internet connectivity is available.
- Accurate AI Predictions: Regularly update the system with reliable sales and seasonal data.
- Efficient Logistics: Partner with local transport providers and use route optimization to reduce delivery delays and costs.
- Quality Monitoring: Introduce proper packaging and timely delivery practices to maintain product freshness.
## Impact and Benefits
Potential Impact on the Target Audience
AgroConnect will positively impact farmers, FPOs, consumers and bulk buyers by providing a direct digital marketplace. Farmers can reach more buyers and improve their earnings, while consumers can access fresh agricultural products at affordable prices. FPOs and retailers can benefit from better market access, demand predictions and efficient delivery services. The platform also supports rural development by encouraging digital adoption in agriculture.

Benefits of the Solution
- Economic Benefits: Improves farmers' earnings, reduces intermediary costs and provides affordable prices to consumers.
- Social Benefits: Empowers farmers through digital access and creates better market opportunities for rural communities.
- Environmental Benefits: Reduces food wastage and fuel consumption through demand forecasting and optimized delivery routes.
- Agricultural Benefits: Helps farmers make informed decisions using AI-based demand predictions and improves supply chain efficiency.
